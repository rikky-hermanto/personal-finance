## The idea in one sentence

Quests should be things the app notices you already did, revealed in one satisfying monthly moment, not a to-do list the app hands you.

---

## What is wrong with quests today

Evidence from the code, not the prototype:

- **They are generated by an LLM each time they are fetched.** [JourneyController.cs](../../apps/api/src/PersonalFinance.Api/Controllers/JourneyController.cs) calls `advisorClient.GenerateQuestsAsync(state)`, and [journey_advisor.py](../../services/ai-service/app/services/journey_advisor.py) asks for "exactly 3 quests targeting the weakest indicators". Nothing is saved.
- **A quest can never be completed.** There is no quest state anywhere. The "✓ Complete" button and "Reset demo" in the screenshot belong to the HTML prototype (`pf-reward.jsx`). The live [QuestCard.tsx](../../apps/frontend/src/components/journey/QuestCard.tsx) has only "Start" and a "Remind me" stub that does nothing.
- **The "+pts" number is made up.** `estimatedScoreGain` is written by the LLM. In the screenshot, "Upload last month's statement, +12 pts, spend lt income" promises a score gain that uploading cannot cause, because uploading does not change how much you spend.
- **The old idea list is mostly homework.** [journey-quest-ideas.md](../../docs/ideas/journey-quest-ideas.md) has about 120 quests. Many are data-entry chores ("log at least 1 insurance policy", "add a note documenting…"), and several steer products or allocations, which the CIO rejects below.

## Design rules: fun, not a burden

1. **The quest finds you.** Most quests complete by themselves from data you already give the app. You never press "Complete" on something the app can see.
2. **Make the one real chore the best moment of the month.** Uploading a statement is unavoidable. It becomes the **Monthly Harvest**: every quest resolves at once, with a short reveal.
3. **Two meters, never mixed.** The **pyramid score** is the truth about your money and moves only when real money moves. The **garden** is your effort and consistency, and it never shrinks. Quests grow the garden. Only real money moves the score.
4. **Never punish.** No wilting, no daily streaks, no nagging push notifications, no leaderboards. A bad month still grows the garden, because hiding bad months would make every score a lie.
5. **Choice, not assignment.** Three quests on offer, you pick one as your focus, and you can swap it any time at no cost.
6. **Stars instead of invented points.** Each indicator earns ⭐ at score 50, ⭐⭐ at 70 (level cleared) and ⭐⭐⭐ at 100 (mastered). These come straight from the rubric's breakpoints, so every star is exactly true.
7. **Small rungs for slow goals.** A three-month emergency fund takes a year to build. Split it into six fence posts of half a month each, so there is something to celebrate every few months.
8. **Burden budget.** At most 10 minutes of deliberate effort a month beyond the upload. Any manual quest takes 2 minutes or less and pays out a reveal immediately ("You are covered for 1.4 months").

## Quest types

| Type | How it completes | Effort | Can it move the score? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | One-time setup that unlocks a reveal (your real monthly spend, your months of cover) | 1–3 min, once | Only by adding real data the score was missing |
| **Ritual** | The Monthly Harvest upload, counted in months, with one free skip per quarter | The upload you already do | No |
| **Milestone** | Detected automatically when an indicator crosses a rung | Zero | It is the score, made visible |
| **Challenge** | Opt-in, time-boxed, measured against your own past average | Zero after opting in | No (behaviour may move it later) |
| **Hidden** | Surprise recognition of something good you did without being asked | Zero | No |
| **Boss** | Level graduation: every live indicator in the level reaches ⭐⭐ (score 70) | Zero | It is the score |
| **Self-check** | You confirm something the app cannot see | One tap | Never (FIN-04) |

## The core loop: Monthly Harvest

```
   You upload last month's statement (the habit you already have)
                          │
                          ▼
   Recalculation runs (already event-driven on TransactionCreatedEvent)
   Quest detectors run in the same pass and store completions as "unseen"
                          │
                          ▼
   Next Journey visit: the Harvest reveal
     • "September harvested · 4 of 5 accounts"   (completeness shown)
     • each completed quest pops in turn, with its garden item
     • stars earned, then a level-cleared moment if one happened
     • a red month still says "Harvested. Your garden grew."
                          │
                          ▼
   Pick next month's focus quest (1 of 3, swap any time)
```

This also answers the open question in [reward-loop-closure.md](../../docs/ideas/reward-loop-closure.md) about quests that complete while the app is closed: completions queue as unseen and play at the next visit.

## Level by level

"Ready" means the data to detect it exists today. "Needs" names the missing piece.

### L1 Foundations: "Clear the field"

The garden starts as an overgrown plot. Each account you add clears a patch, and green months plant the first seeds.

- **Live indicator:** `spend_lt_income` (3-month average of expense ÷ income). `pay_bills_on_time` is not available yet.
- **Stars:** ⭐ ratio 0.95 · ⭐⭐ 0.89 · ⭐⭐⭐ 0.80
- **Boss, "Clear the Field":** 3-month average spending at or below 89% of income.

| Quest | Type | How it completes | Effort | Ready? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meet your money | Discovery | First statement uploaded. Reveal: your real monthly in and out, and your #1 spending category | 3 min | Ready |
| Sort the mystery pile | Discovery | Top 5 uncategorized merchants given a category. Reveal: your spending by category | 2 min | Ready |
| Full picture | Discovery, once per account | Each account you spend from is uploaded at least once. Each one clears a patch of the field | 2 min per account | Ready |
| Monthly Harvest | Ritual | Last month uploaded. Counted in months, one free skip per quarter | Existing habit | Ready |
| In the green | Milestone | Any single month where spending is below income | Zero | Ready, but unfair to savers until fix G3 |
| Tanggal Tua Survivor (*tanggal tua* is the lean week before payday) | Challenge | In the 7 days before salary lands, you spend less than your own weekly average | Zero | Ready |
| Trim one | Challenge | You pick one non-essential category and spend 10% below your own 3-month average for it | Zero | Ready |
| No-spend weekend | Challenge | One weekend with no discretionary spending | Zero | Ready |
| Honest Harvest | Hidden | You upload a month where you overspent | Zero | Ready |
| Bounce Back | Hidden | A green month right after a red one | Zero | Ready |
| Bills in the basket | Milestone | Every recurring payment that Buckets already infers shows up as paid this month | Zero | Needs a bills indicator built on Buckets commitments |

### L2 Defense: "Build the fence"

A fence goes up one post at a time (emergency fund). Rocks in the field are debts, and each one cleared is a moment.

- **Live indicators:** `liquid_savings_ratio` (months of cover) and `manageable_dti` (debt payments ÷ income).
- **Stars, cover:** ⭐ 1.5 months · ⭐⭐ 2.1 months · ⭐⭐⭐ 3 months
- **Stars, debt:** ⭐ 36% · ⭐⭐ 29.6% · ⭐⭐⭐ 20%
- **Boss, "The Fence Holds":** at least 2.1 months of cover and debt payments at or below 29.6% of income.

| Quest | Type | How it completes | Effort | Ready? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Count your cushion | Discovery | Savings balances confirmed. Reveal: "You are covered for X months" | 1 min | Ready |
| Map your debts | Discovery | The app spots *cicilan* (instalment), paylater or card payments in your transactions and asks "Add this?". Or you tap "I have no debts" | 1 tap each | Manual part ready. Auto-spotting needs keyword detection (small) |
| Fence posts | Milestone ladder | One post for each half month of cover, six posts up to 3 months | Zero | Ready, with guardrail G6 |
| Lighter load | Milestone | DTI stars as above | Zero | Ready after fix G4 |
| Rock cleared | Milestone | A debt's payments stop and you answer "Paid off or paused?" | 1 tap | Ready, with guardrail G5 |
| Refilled | Hidden | Your cover dropped by at least one post and came back. Using the fund is what it is for | Zero | Ready |
| Untouched | Hidden | Cover never dipped for 6 months | Zero | Ready |
| Most expensive rock first | Challenge | Extra payment on your highest-interest debt (liabilities already store `interest_rate`) | Zero | Later |
| Pay yourself first | Challenge | Money moves to savings within 3 days of payday | Zero | Needs transfer detection ([money-tracing.md](../../docs/ideas/money-tracing.md)) |
| Shield check | Discovery | BPJS Kesehatan and other policies recorded with coverage | 2 min | Needs insurance records |

### L3 Growth: "Plant the orchard"

Saplings take years. Each holding you already own plants one. Contributions are rain.

- **Live indicator:** `savings_rate` (investment and savings contributions ÷ income, 3-month average).
- **Stars:** ⭐ 5% · ⭐⭐ 9% · ⭐⭐⭐ 15%
- **Boss, "First Harvest":** 3-month average contributions of at least 9% of income.
- **Gate (CIO):** before L2 is cleared, only recognition quests appear. Quests that ask you to start or raise investing appear only after L2 is cleared and debt has been evaluated.
- **Blocked by fix G3.** Today, investing raises your L1 spending ratio, so an L3 quest would lower your L1 score.

| Quest | Type | How it completes | Effort | Ready? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Count your trees | Discovery, any level | Investments you already hold are recorded. Each one plants a sapling | 2 min | Ready |
| Name a goal | Discovery | One goal with an amount and a date in the Buckets Future plan | 1 min | Ready |
| First seed | Milestone | First month with a contribution | Zero | Ready after G3 |
| Autopilot | Hidden | About the same amount on about the same day for 3 months running | Zero | Ready after G3 |
| Steady rain | Milestone | Contributions in 6 consecutive months | Zero | Ready after G3 |
| Windfall rule | Seasonal | When income lands well above your usual (THR, the religious-holiday bonus, or another bonus), it is split the way you chose in advance | Zero after setup | Ready after G3 |

Things grow while you sleep. Most of this level waits for passive-income data, so it is mostly recognition.

- **Live indicators:** none. `passive_income` is not available yet, so there is no boss yet.

## What we will not do

| Not doing | Why |
|---|---|
| Daily check-ins or daily streaks | Finance runs monthly. Daily pressure is exactly the burden you want to avoid |
| Push nagging ("your seedling is thirsty", from [gamification-engine.md](../../docs/ideas/gamification-engine.md) Phase 4) | Nagging turns the app into a chore |
| Wilting or decay after bad months (also gamification-engine.md Phase 4) | People stop uploading bad months, and then every score is wrong |
| Leaderboards or comparisons with other people | Money shame, and a privacy risk |
| Quests that need logging each purchase | Per-purchase capture is the habit that failed the cash-reconciliation analysis (2026-10-24) |
| More than 3 quests visible | Choice overload |
| Quest points added to the health score | The score stops meaning anything |
| The "Remind me" button | Replace it with "Not now", which hides the quest for a month |

## Risks & Blind Spots

- **Assumption risk:** the monthly upload is the real rhythm. If uploads stop, the harvest stops too. The free skip and the absence of any penalty keep the way back open.
- **Scope creep risk:** garden art, the three themes in gamification-engine.md (forest, city, farm), an XP economy, and seasonal live events. The garden visuals are the most tempting and should wait until the engine is proven.
- **Technical dependency:** fixes G3 and G4; a quest state table; transfer detection for "Pay yourself first"; passive-income and insurance data for parts of L2 and L4.
- **User behaviour risk:** low. The harvest rides on the upload habit that already exists, and no new habit is required.
- **Gaming risk:** relabeling an account as Savings, or categorizing transfers as investments. In a single-user app this is only self-deception. Guardrail G6 (fresh balances) prevents accidental fake wins.

## Verdict

**Go, phased. PM and CIO agree, with the CIO's guardrails below.**

Build the self-completing quest engine for L1 and L2 first. The decisive reason is that the home page's "what next" is currently decorative: quests are regenerated on every fetch, can never be finished, and show score gains an LLM made up. Replacing that with a small deterministic catalog makes Journey honest and more fun at the same moment, and it costs less to run. Before you build, check one thing: after the first month of harvests, does opening the reveal feel like a reward to you? If it feels like a report card, the copy and pacing need work before more quests are added.

- **Suggested ticket:** "Journey quests v2: self-completing L1/L2 quests with stars"
- **First acceptance criterion:** after an upload that moves the 3-month spend ratio below 0.95, the "In the green ⭐" quest shows as completed on the next Journey load, stays completed after a reload, and the L1 score equals what recalculation gives with quests switched off.
- **Next step:** run `/plan journey-quests-v2` to produce the implementation plan.

---

## Alternative Framings

1. **Stars only, no quests.** Ship only stars on indicators plus the harvest reveal. It is the cheapest option and may be enough on its own. The quest catalog comes later if stars feel thin.
2. **Advisor-led.** No quest UI at all. The AI advisor writes one short note after each harvest saying what went well and what to try next. More personal, but it needs strict number guardrails, and the advisor's tool round trip is currently broken on Gemini 3.
3. **Seasons first.** Lead with the calendar events (Lebaran, THR, 11.11, Year in Review) as limited-time challenges. High excitement, but the fun becomes seasonal instead of monthly.

# CIO Review: Journey Quests

### What this actually is

In investment terms, this is **behavioural coaching delivered as a game**. Vanguard's *Advisor's Alpha* work says the largest part of the value a professional adds is behavioural: keeping clients saving, sequencing their goals correctly, and stopping them abandoning the plan after a bad period. That is what quests are for. The need underneath is not entertainment. It is **adherence to a correctly ordered plan**, and the fun is the delivery mechanism.

### Where it sits in the pyramid

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels served | L1–L5, with real substance in L1–L3 |
| Level a user must reach for each money-moving prompt to be good advice | Building cover needs L1 in the green. Starting or raising investing needs L2 cleared and no unevaluated debt |
| Does the product enforce that today? | **Partially.** `DetermineCurrentLevel` ([JourneyScoringService.cs:348-366](../../apps/api/src/PersonalFinance.Application/Services/JourneyScoringService.cs#L348-L366)) returns 1 both for a user who has not cleared L1 and for one who has. Quest gating must use the first level not yet cleared, not `currentLevel` |

If a user at L1 receives "start investing" quests, they are investing at a negative cash-flow position, often while carrying paylater debt that costs far more than any expected investment return. That is the trap the design's gate prevents. The gate is correct and must be enforced in code, not left to copy.

### The professional-defensibility test

1. **Is the mechanism real?** Yes. Pre-commitment (the windfall rule), automatic contribution (Autopilot), and sequencing (the pyramid gate) are the best-evidenced behavioural levers in household finance. "Honest Harvest" protects data completeness, which every score depends on.
2. **What must be assumed?** That the uploaded accounts are the whole picture, that savings balances are current, that categories are right, and that the rubric's breakpoints are sensible for Indonesia. Today only the first is invisible to the user; guardrail G10 makes it visible.
3. **How is it wrong when it's wrong?** It celebrates something false: a stale savings balance, a debt "paid off" that was actually missed, a perfect debt score with no debts recorded. Each one has a guardrail below.
4. **Would I sign it?** Yes, with the guardrails and the two fixes. Without fix G3 I would not sign any L3 quest, because the product would punish the behaviour it asks for.

### Methodology findings (from the code, not the docs)

🔴 **Investing counts as spending.** The seed presets type `REKSA DANA`, `SAHAM`, `OBLIGASI`, `BIBIT`, `AJAIB`, `BAREKSA` and `STOCKBIT` as Expense ([20260510000002_seed_category_presets.sql:101-107](../../supabase/migrations/20260510000002_seed_category_presets.sql#L101-L107)). `ComputeSpendLtIncomeAsync` sums every Expense row ([JourneyScoringService.cs:110-113](../../apps/api/src/PersonalFinance.Application/Services/JourneyScoringService.cs#L110-L113)). Failing input: income Rp 10 jt, consumption Rp 8 jt, investing Rp 1.5 jt. The code computes a ratio of 0.95, which scores 50 and does not clear L1. The correct consumption ratio is 0.80, which scores 100. [formulas.md §1](../../docs/reference/finance-domain/formulas.md) defines savings rate as `(income − expense) / income`, which only works if expense means consumption. **Fix:** exclude the savings-rate categories (`Investment`, `Savings Transfer`, `Investasi`, `Tabungan`) from the expense side of `spend_lt_income`. Dividends stay as income.

🔴 **No debt data scores as perfect (FIN-04).** With no liabilities recorded, `totalMonthlyPayment` is 0, DTI is 0, and the score is 100 ([JourneyScoringService.cs:207-230](../../apps/api/src/PersonalFinance.Application/Services/JourneyScoringService.cs#L207-L230)). The "Light Footprint" achievement then unlocks ([:380](../../apps/api/src/PersonalFinance.Application/Services/JourneyScoringService.cs#L380)). Green without checking is exactly what FIN-04 forbids. **Fix:** DTI stays "not evaluated" until at least one liability is recorded or the user confirms "I have no debts". The "Map your debts" quest is the natural way to collect that confirmation.

⚠ **Graduation numbers differ from the headlines.** Clearing a level requires every live indicator at 70 or above, which means spending at about 89% of income for L1, about 2.1 months of cover and about 29.6% DTI for L2, and about 9% savings rate for L3. CLAUDE.md headlines say 3 months, under 20%, and at least 15%. The 70 bar is defensible: you do not need a full three months of cover before starting to invest, as long as high-interest debt is gone. But it is undocumented. **Recommendation:** keep the scoring and name the two bars. ⭐⭐ (70) is **Cleared**, and ⭐⭐⭐ (100) is **Mastered**. Update the CLAUDE.md pyramid headlines to say which bar they describe.

⚠ **DTI uses take-home income.** [formulas.md §1](../../docs/reference/finance-domain/formulas.md) defines DTI on gross income. The code divides by income seen in bank statements, which is net, so DTI reads higher than the 36% convention assumes. That is conservative, which is the safe direction, but it is unstated. Add one line to the rubric.

⚠ **Savings balances can be stale.** Cover uses the latest valuation of each Savings account regardless of its date ([:153-166](../../apps/api/src/PersonalFinance.Application/Services/JourneyScoringService.cs#L153-L166)). A fence post must not be celebrated from a six-month-old balance (guardrail G6).

⚠ **Achievement names claim more than the rule checks.** `positive_cashflow_3mo` unlocks when the current 3-month average scores at least 70 ([:378](../../apps/api/src/PersonalFinance.Application/Services/JourneyScoringService.cs#L378)), not after three consecutive positive months. Rename it, or make the rule match the name.

⚠ **Insurance is in the wrong level.** The rubric and code put `appropriate_insurance` in L3. Protection belongs in L2 Defense: a hospital bill without cover empties the emergency fund. It is not scored yet, so moving it now changes nobody's score. Move it now, while the move is free.

🟢 **Two meters.** Keeping the health score out of reach of effort points is correct. A score you can buy with clicks stops measuring health (Goodhart's law). Do not let anyone merge them later "for simplicity".

🟢 **Honest Harvest.** This is the most important finance decision in the design. A system that punishes bad months makes users hide them, and then every average is biased. Reward completeness, never selection.

🟢 **"Years bought" as a plain ratio.** Investable assets ÷ a year of current spending needs no withdrawal-rate assumption, so it is honest by construction. It becomes dishonest the moment a rung is labeled "free". [formulas.md §7](../../docs/reference/finance-domain/formulas.md) explains why the 4% rule does not carry over to Indonesia.

🟢 **The rejections.** Product quests (log an SBN, log a mutual fund, crypto), allocation targets (at least 60% equity, no class above 80%), counting asset classes as "diversification", and return-based quests are all correctly out. Counting asset classes rewards owning more things, not lower risk. Return quests reward risk and luck. Allocation belongs in the user's own mandate (the Desk mandate), not in a game.

### Cost, tax and drag

None of the proposed quests encourages trading, so the design adds no trading drag. Autopilot and Steady rain reward regular contribution, which reduces timing risk at no cost. The SPT quest exports data and must quote no rates; tax rates are perishable (FIN-06). "Most expensive rock first" is the one quest with certain, measurable value: paying down the highest-rate debt is a guaranteed return equal to that rate.

### CIO verdict

**BUILD WITH GUARDRAILS.** This is behavioural coaching, the most defensible thing a retail wealth product can do, and the design already rejects the quests a fiduciary would refuse. The decisive condition is that the scores the game celebrates must be true first. Fixes G3 and G4 are prerequisites, and L3 quests are **not yet** until G3 lands. What would change my mind: any proposal to let quest completion add points to the health score.

**Guardrails, written as acceptance criteria:**

- **G1.** Completing any quest leaves `user_journey_state.indicator_scores` and `level_scores` unchanged. Only indicator recalculation changes them.
- **G2.** Every number on a quest card is computed from rubric breakpoints in code. No LLM output may contain a number shown to the user. Remove `estimatedScoreGain` from the LLM schema.
- **G3.** `spend_lt_income` excludes the savings-rate categories from expense. Ship this before any L2 cover quest or L3 quest goes live, and record it in the rubric as a methodology change.
- **G4.** `manageable_dti` returns "not evaluated" when no liability is recorded and the user has not confirmed "no debts". `debt_free` cannot unlock from an empty liabilities table.
- **G5.** "Rock cleared" never completes automatically when payments stop. The user chooses "Paid off" or "Paused / missed", and "Paused / missed" shows a calm help path with no celebration.
- **G6.** A fence post completes only when the savings balance is dated within the harvested month (taken from the statement's closing balance where one is available).
- **G7.** Quests that ask you to move money are gated on the first level not yet cleared, not on `currentLevel`. "Start or raise investing" stays hidden until L2 is cleared and DTI has been evaluated.
- **G8.** A catalog test fails if any quest names a product, issuer, asset class, allocation percentage, return figure or trading frequency.
- **G9.** "Years bought" is labeled "at your current spending", and no rung is named "free" or "retired". Any FI number shows the 3%, 4% and 5% withdrawal rates and passes `/compliance` before shipping.
- **G10.** The harvest result states completeness: "based on N of M accounts".
- **G11.** Move `appropriate_insurance` to L2 in the rubric and the code while it is still not scored.

### Blast radius of the two fixes

- **G3** lowers the L1 spending ratio for anyone with investment or savings outflows, so L1 scores only go **up**. Nobody loses an L1 clearance. Rows already in `journey_indicator_snapshots` were computed under the old rule. Note the rule change in the rubric (a version bump, as its Audit Trail section requires) rather than rewriting history.
- **G4** removes a fake 100 from L2 for any user with no liabilities recorded. L2's average can drop. Clearance cannot flip from this alone, because removing an indicator that scored 100 cannot make "all at 70 or above" false. Check whether the owner's "Light Footprint" achievement was earned on empty data. If it was, keep the record and let the owner decide, rather than deleting a celebrated moment silently.
